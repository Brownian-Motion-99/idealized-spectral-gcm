**F1 repaired: accepted-state grid vorticity and divergence**

The primitive-equation coupling now reconstructs `grid_vor` and `grid_div`
after physics projects the adjusted winds into spectral space. Both diagnostic
arrays describe the accepted next state before time cycling and the accepted
current state afterward. The next dynamics step therefore uses divergence and
vorticity consistent with its winds.

Both high-level restart paths also reconstruct these diagnostics from loaded
current spectra before resumed dynamics. The checkpoint's prognostics, previous
time level, and saved time remain unchanged.

The original [static audit](gcm_static_audit_20261006.md), its recorded
observations, and source manifest provide the pre-repair evidence. This report
records the October 6 validation; the reusable regression tests and validation
harnesses are retained alongside its aggregate results and source provenance.

**Implementation**

- `src/Dynamics/Spectral_Dynamics.jl`: a private helper performs the two
  sequential inverse transforms into existing diagnostic buffers. The helper
  is used after post-physics wind projection and at the existing provisional
  dynamics reconstruction site.
- `src/Initialization/Initial_Conditions.jl`: a shared high-level restart
  loader reconstructs the primitive-equation diagnostics from `spe_vor_c`
  and `spe_div_c`. The public initializer uses this loader.
- `src/Driver.jl`: the warm-start branch uses the same loader. This is the
  only change to the driver's pre-existing working-tree edits.

The conservation correction order, wind projection, physical parameterizations,
prognostic advancement, checkpoint format, and raw array-loader semantics are
preserved. Absolute vorticity already gets reconstructed before its momentum
consumer. Other audit findings, including the saved omega timing offset, remain
separate work.

Inspection corrected an assumption in the original workplan: the present PBL
kernel mixes temperature and humidity, not horizontal momentum. Its
synchronization path is tested; HS drag supplies the wind-changing regression.

The observations record water, dry mass, `cp*T + Lv*q + kinetic energy`, mean

**Performance**

Sequential one-thread runs, excluding the first four steps from timing:

| Case | Baseline median step | Fixed median step |
|:---|---:|---:|
| BM off | `0.14964 s` | `0.15325 s` |
| BM on | `0.36396 s` | `0.36316 s` |

The isolated refresh costs approximately `1.9–2.7 ms` and allocates
`2,816 bytes` in small transform/task bookkeeping. It allocates no new
full-grid buffer; one T42L20 Float64 field occupies `1,310,720 bytes`.
BM-off full-step allocations increase by exactly `2,816 bytes`.

Whole-step timing also reflects scheduler variation and the changed subsequent
trajectory/convective branches. The slightly faster BM-on median is not evidence
that the added transforms accelerate physics. These measurements establish a
small direct refresh cost, not a general performance guarantee.
